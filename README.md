# 🌧️⚡ RainVolt – Rooftop Rainwater Micro-Hydro Energy System

> **Turning Rainwater into Renewable Energy**

RainVolt is a rooftop rainwater micro-hydro energy prototype designed to combine **rainwater harvesting and renewable electricity generation**.

The system captures rainwater from a rooftop, directs it through a pipe and splitter valve, and uses the water flow to drive a micro-turbine. The generated energy can be stored in a battery and used for small electrical loads.

## 🚀 Key Features

* 🌧️ Rainwater collection from rooftops
* 💧 Controlled water flow through piping
* ⚙️ Micro-turbine based electricity generation
* 🔋 Battery energy storage
* 💡 Small-load power demonstration
* 🚰 Water storage for reuse
* 🔀 Automatic bypass during heavy rainfall
* 📊 Live simulation of rainfall and energy generation
* 🌱 Sustainable and eco-friendly concept

## 🔄 How RainVolt Works

```text
Rainfall
   ↓
Rooftop
   ↓
Collection Pipe
   ↓
Splitter Valve
   ├──→ Micro-Turbine → Generator → Battery → Load
   │
   └──→ Storage Tank / Overflow Drain
```

The collected rainwater is directed through the micro-turbine when suitable flow conditions are available. During excessive rainfall, the bypass path prevents excessive water flow through the turbine.

## ⚡ Energy Generation

The prototype estimates hydroelectric power using:

**P = ρ × g × Q × H × η**

Where:

* **P** = Generated power
* **ρ** = Water density
* **g** = Gravitational acceleration
* **Q** = Water flow rate
* **H** = Effective water head
* **η** = Overall turbine-generator efficiency
